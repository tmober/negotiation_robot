Robot negotiation task: chat prototype

This prototype runs the Robot negotiation task from Martin-Raugh et al. (2020) entirely in a chat. The test-taker negotiates with Sam, a teammate played by AI. A scripted Task guide gives the instructions and, after the negotiation, asks the questions.
